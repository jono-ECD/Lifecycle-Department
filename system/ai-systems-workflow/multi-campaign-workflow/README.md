# Multi-Campaign Work Flow

Planning, building, and shipping a campaign calendar across accounts: promo calendar, segmentation, build, schedule, and post-send review. The highest-frequency motion the department runs, so small efficiencies compound hardest here.

## Inputs
- Promo and content calendar
- Segment strategy per send
- Creative assets
- Sending cadence and fatigue constraints

## Outputs
- Built and scheduled campaigns
- Segment assignments and exclusions
- Post-send performance review

## Depends on
`creative-workflows` for assets; `qa-workflow` before scheduling; `clients/registry.yml` for account routing.
