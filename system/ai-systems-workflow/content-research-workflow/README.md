# Content & Research Work Flow

Gathering and synthesizing the inputs strategy depends on: voice-of-customer, competitive scan, category trends, and review mining. Automating this is what makes strategy work affordable at account scale.

## Inputs
- Review, support, and survey corpora
- Competitor and category sources
- Specific research question — not a general sweep

## Outputs
- Synthesized research brief with cited sources
- Voice-of-customer language bank
- Claims and objections inventory

## Depends on
`agent-skills`. Feeds `marketing-strategy/target-audience` and `customer-profile-persona`.
