# Customer Profile / Persona

Persona work that earns its place by changing messaging decisions. Tied to real profile attributes and behaviors so a persona maps to a queryable cohort, not a stock photo and a first name.

## Inputs
- Target audience definition
- Behavioral and purchase data
- Voice-of-customer research

## Outputs
- Personas mapped to queryable profile attributes
- Messaging angles and objections per persona
- Channel and cadence preferences per persona

## Depends on
`target-audience` — personas subdivide the audience; they do not replace defining it.
