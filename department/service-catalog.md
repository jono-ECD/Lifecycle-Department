What the Lifecycle & Retention Department sells and delivers. Each service maps to

## Onboarding & Migration

| Service | Scope | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Full Implementation | End-to-end lifecycle build: integration, full flow architecture, segmentation, deliverability, handoff | [`onboarding-migration.full-implementation`](../skills/onboarding-migration/full-implementation/SKILL.md) |
| Mini Implementation | Core revenue flows only — a scoped subset of Full, never a fork of it | [`onboarding-migration.mini-implementation`](../skills/onboarding-migration/mini-implementation/SKILL.md) |
| SMS Migration | Move an SMS program into Klaviyo with consent provenance and carrier registration intact | [`onboarding-migration.sms-migration`](../skills/onboarding-migration/sms-migration/SKILL.md) |
| Marketing Analytics (Personalization) | Event tracking, catalog integration, custom properties — the measurement substrate | [`onboarding-migration.marketing-analytics-personalization`](../skills/onboarding-migration/marketing-analytics-personalization/SKILL.md) |
| Customer Agent | Klaviyo AI customer agent: knowledge, skills, tools, escalation | [`onboarding-migration.customer-agent`](../skills/onboarding-migration/customer-agent/SKILL.md) |
| K:Social | Klaviyo Social setup and audience integration | [`onboarding-migration.k-social`](../skills/onboarding-migration/k-social/SKILL.md) |
