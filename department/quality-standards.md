# Quality standards

The minimum bar for any deliverable, regardless of which skill produced it. Skills
add their own checks; none may lower these.

## Factual integrity

- Every claim traces to account context or an approved source.
- Offer terms — amount, code, dates, restrictions — match the brief exactly.
  Never inferred, never remembered from a previous campaign.
- Figures in reporting are traceable to a named source and date range.
- Time-sensitive facts are re-confirmed if the confirmation is stale relative to
  the send date.

## Compliance

- No claim that the account's `messaging-constraints.md` prohibits.
- Required disclosures present.
- SMS: consent basis valid, opt-out present, quiet hours respected.
- Regulated categories (health, supplements, pharma, financial) go to the named
  reviewer before shipping.

## Craft

- One primary CTA per message, phrased as an action.
- Every personalization token has a fallback that reads naturally when empty.
- Links resolve and point where the brief says.
- Voice matches the account's `brand-voice.md`.

## Process

- The QA gate ran and its result is recorded.
- A named human reviewed before anything sends.
- Skill versions used are recorded with the deliverable.
- Deliverables are saved under the correct account folder.

## The account check

Confirm the account before doing anything else. Applying one account's context to
another is the most damaging error available here, and it is silent — the output
looks entirely plausible.
