## Purpose

Pull performance, compare against the retention thesis targets, surface the bottleneck, and draft the narrative. Turns \`marketing-strategy.account-growth-analysis\` from a manual monthly scramble into a standing process.

## When to use

On the account's reporting cadence, or when a performance threshold is breached. **Requires a live data connector** — check \`accounts/registry.yml\` first and say so plainly if none exists.

## Inputs

- Performance data (Klaviyo, Hiro, platform)
- Targets from the account retention thesis
- Prior period results and test history
