## Purpose

Gather and synthesize the inputs strategy depends on: voice-of-customer, competitive scan, category trends, and review mining. Automating this is what makes strategy work affordable at account scale.

## When to use

A strategy skill needs evidence it does not have — before persona, audience, or messaging work, not after.

## Inputs

- Review, support, and survey corpora
- Competitor and category sources
- A specific research question, not a general sweep
