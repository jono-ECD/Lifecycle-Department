## Purpose

Translate a purchase journey into a concrete flow build: triggers, filters, timing, branching, and message sequence. The bridge between strategy and implementation, and the highest leverage per hour invested.

## When to use

A journey gap has been identified and a flow needs specifying, or an existing flow needs re-architecting.

## Inputs

- Purchase journey map with gaps identified
- Retention thesis and priority cohorts
- Existing flow inventory
- Catalog and replenishment model
